# Hero styles

[`hero.css`](hero.css) styles the Pangaea hero image and text, CTA panel, and social proof sections.

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://engineeringtrainer.github.io/pangaea-assets/css/hero.css">
```

Use the matching `et-hero`, `et-hero-cta`, `et-socialproof-facts`, and `et-socialproof-logos` classes on the page. Add `et-hero-image-cover` for the image overlay. The stylesheet also provides `section-padding-none` and `section-padding-bottom-none` spacing helpers.

CTA buttons fill their container at 640px and below. Social proof fact dividers change at 641px and 1200px; logo carousel controls are hidden.
